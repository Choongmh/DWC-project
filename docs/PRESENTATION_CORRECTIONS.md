# Corrections for future presentations

1. Slide 9 says the water cap came from the last eight quarters. The final calculation uses **nine quarter-ending observations**, January 2006 through January 2008 inclusive. The cap is 13,247.5803 STB/day and is an assumption, not a measured facility specification.
2. Slide 10 calls P50 'most likely'. Use **median of ten selected scenarios**. A median is not generally a mode, and these ten cases are not a calibrated probability sample.
3. Slide 8's 19% average comparison mixes proxy-estimated and simulator scores. Present it as a descriptive comparison, not verified optimization improvement.
4. Slide 9's cold test is not independent validation of the full forecasting process. The water diagnostic uses known future proxy oil and case selection used the full history.
5. Slide 2's profitability question is business motivation. The project does not calculate revenue, costs, tax, discount rates or NPV, so it cannot conclude whether the field makes money.
6. The Power BI cards use maximum cumulative values in their current filter context. An unfiltered maximum across cases is not P50. Use explicit date and scenario filters and the workbook's median measure when presenting medians.
7. Distinguish cumulative mismatch from rate mismatch. Small cumulative errors can coexist with the reported 12–15% late oil-rate bias.

Original slides remain intact as historical evidence. Apply these corrections when creating a new personal presentation.
